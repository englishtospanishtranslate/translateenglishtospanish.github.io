Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about this translator.

Is this translator really free?

Yes. The translator and all tools on this site are free to use with no account. Translation is powered by a free community translation service, so a fair-use daily limit may apply on very heavy use.

How accurate is the translation?

For everyday sentences, travel phrases, messages and homework it is very reliable. Idioms, slang and legal or medical wording can need a human touch — for official documents, always use a certified translator.

Can I hear how the Spanish sounds?

Yes. After translating, press the “Listen” button and your browser will read the translation aloud using its built-in Spanish voice.

Is my text stored anywhere?

Your text is sent only to the translation service to produce the result. We do not keep copies of what you translate on this website.
