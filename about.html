About Us

What this site is, and who it is for.

This site is a free language toolkit for anyone who needs to move between English and Spanish — students, travellers, professionals, and Spanish learners everywhere.

What you can do here

Translate text instantly between English and Spanish, hear it read aloud for pronunciation practice, count words and characters before sending a document for professional translation, and type Spanish accented characters without a Spanish keyboard. Alongside the tools, our guides explain the tricky parts of Spanish — formal and informal address, false friends, common phrases — in plain English.

How the translator works

Translations are produced by a free community translation service and delivered to your browser. Nothing you type is published on this site. Machine translation is excellent for everyday communication, but for legal, medical, immigration or other official documents we always recommend a certified human translator.

Our standard

Every guide on this site is written to be accurate, original and genuinely useful. If you spot an error, please tell us through the contact page — corrections are made quickly.
