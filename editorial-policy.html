Editorial Policy

How our guides are written and checked.

Original content

Every guide published on this site is written originally for this site. We do not copy articles from other websites or republish machine-translated content as our own writing.

Accuracy

Spanish examples in our guides are checked against standard usage (RAE-aligned). Regional variation is real — where a word differs between Spain and Latin America, we try to say so rather than present one region's usage as universal.

Corrections

Language questions often have more than one right answer. If you believe a guide is wrong or incomplete, contact us with the example and your reasoning; verified corrections are published promptly.

Advertising

If ads appear on this site, they never influence what our guides say. Tool recommendations inside guides are based on usefulness, not sponsorship.
