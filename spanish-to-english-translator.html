Tips for translating Spanish into English

Spanish often drops the subject pronoun because the verb ending already shows who is acting. The translator handles this well in full sentences, so paste complete sentences whenever you can. Watch out for “false friends” — words that look English but mean something else: embarazada means pregnant (not embarrassed), actual means current (not actual), and librería is a bookshop (not a library).

Accents matter in Spanish: sí (yes) vs si (if), or tú (you) vs tu (your). If you are typing Spanish without a Spanish keyboard, use our Spanish Accents Tool to add the correct characters before translating.
